# 城市风景海报（City Editorial Poster）技能

## 风格定义
将城市/风景真实照片转换为竖版（2:3）的高级编辑海报，整体呈现独立艺术杂志内页、城市旅行摄影集与当代艺术书籍封面的视觉质感。

构图采用严格的上下两部分分区：
- **上半部分（约占 50%）**：保留原始真实摄影画面，不做 AI 重绘。仅做轻微电影感调色：降低饱和度、柔和色调、以蓝灰/暖黄/米白等低饱和自然色为主，保持真实摄影质感。保留原照片的构图、天空、建筑、灯光、云层、月亮或主体元素。
- **下半部分（约占 50%）**：基于上方照片，将其中最具辨识度元素提炼为极简艺术插画（几何色块 + 横向柔软笔触 + 少量线条 + 新月 + 暖黄灯光点缀）。插画质感为 soft gouache / dry brush / 水彩半透明 / 轻微纸张颗粒 / 手绘编辑插画 / 哑光印刷。
- **底部文字排版**：极简杂志式英文标题 + 副标题，主标题优雅经典衬线深蓝灰色，副标题细腻斜体 serif，左对齐，克制字号，足够留白。

## 输出要求
- 输出比例：2:3 竖构图（推荐尺寸 832*1248 或 768*1152）
- 上下分区：各占约 50% 画布，中间有柔和视觉过渡
- 上半：真实摄影电影感调色，低饱和自然色，不过度 HDR、不强锐化、无 AI 重绘感
- 下半：极简几何块插画，soft gouache + 纸张颗粒，无明显描边、不卡通、非赛博朋克、非 3D、非矢量图标感
- 整体配色：warm ivory / muted navy / blue grey / smoky grey / warm ochre / mustard yellow / soft beige / low saturation
- 大量米白色留白，构图疏朗、平衡、安静、诗意、现代
- 底部杂志式衬线英文排版（主标题 + 副标题）

## 生成提示词
Vertical editorial poster, 2:3 ratio, split composition — top half and bottom half.

Top half (50%): Preserve the original real photograph composition exactly. Cinematic color grading only: desaturate, muted and quiet palette in blue-grey, warm yellow, warm ivory. Keep real photo texture, natural sky, architectural structure, lights, clouds, moon, or main subject unchanged. No HDR, no strong sharpening, no AI repaint feel.

Bottom half (50%): Distill the most recognizable elements from the photo above into a minimalist abstract gouache illustration. Buildings reduced to geometric color blocks, far distance compressed into tiny rectangular silhouettes, clouds become soft horizontal brush strokes, sky and ground with few lines, moon simplified to a small crescent, city lights hinted with sparse warm ochre dots. Soft gouache, dry brush texture, watercolor-like opacity, subtle paper grain, hand-painted editorial illustration, matte printed texture. No visible outlines, no cartoon, no cyberpunk, no 3D, no vector icons. Generous warm ivory negative space, balanced and quiet composition.

Bottom typography: Minimal magazine-style English layout. Main title in elegant classic serif font, muted navy blue grey. Subtitle in fine italic serif editorial. Left-aligned, restrained size, generous breathing room.

Overall aesthetic: independent art magazine spread, urban travel photobook, contemporary art book cover, museum shop poster. The bottom illustration must clearly read as an artistic distillation of the photograph above, not a mechanical tracing. Premium, restrained, quiet, poetic, printed paper texture. No commercial advertising feel, no cheap AI look, no excessive decoration. Vertical poster, 2:3 ratio, high resolution, premium editorial composition.
